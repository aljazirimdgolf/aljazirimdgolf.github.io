Before the first tee

How I got here.

Medical training

I earned my MD at American University of Antigua. In interventional psychiatry and TMS, I learned the value of setting a baseline and looking for change over repeated sessions.

TPI Medical Level 2

My TPI training gave me a golf-specific way to think about movement and how differently two golfers can solve the same shot.

Holey Club

I founded and ran Holey Club, an indoor golf facility in Norwich, Connecticut. The simulator makes feedback immediate. The challenge is getting that feedback to matter when you’re standing on the course.

What I coach

Trust you can
take to the course.

I want you to pull a club knowing why it’s right for that shot. If you miss, your job is to learn what you can and trust yourself with the next decision. We start on the range with the Quad and finish in the hole on the course.
